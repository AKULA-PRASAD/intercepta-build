# INTERCEPTA — Flagship actionable targets (the vision's output product)

*The concrete bridge from validated method to real-world action. This is NOT another experiment — it synthesizes the
programme's committed, reproduced-×2, experimentally-anchored results into one decision-grade list: **what to actually work
on, and how fast it could move.** Every target below is validated; the ranking is by REAL-WORLD ACTIONABILITY — targets that
are both validated AND already have a drug can be tested by an experimental group essentially now; validated-but-undrugged
targets need chemistry/funding (the F4 ceiling). Prepared 2026-08-07. Honest tier per row; no clinical claim.*

## How to read this
- **Validation** = the evidence the target matters (essentiality / dependency, with the committed experiment).
- **Intervention readiness** = INTERVENE1/2 result: does a real drug already hit this target or a close homolog?
  - 🟢 **REPURPOSING-READY** — an approved/known drug hits it → a wet-lab group can run a direct test now.
  - 🟡 **UNDRUGGED, HIGH-VALUE** — validated but no existing drug → de-novo chemistry / funding (the honest ceiling).

## Arm A — Broad-spectrum antibacterial targets (WHO priority pathogens)
*Validated across 6 curated + 2 held-out organisms (CROSSVAL, VAL-ESS, prospective-blind SUITE BLIND1-7 [across all 3 domains of life; ~4-5 pass / 2 fail with a mapped transfer boundary]); multi-axis ranked
(BESTINT1); resistance- and condition-robust; host-safety-filtered. All are experimentally essential in ≥2 organisms.*

| Target | Validation (committed) | Intervention readiness |
|---|---|---|
| **murA** | cell-wall core; exp-essential 3/3 orgs (PREDVAL); chokepoint | 🟢 **REPURPOSING-READY** — fosfomycin (approved) targets MurA (INTERVENE1 recovered this) |
| **dxr / ispC** | MEP pathway; exp-essential ≥2/3; multi-axis top (BESTINT1 0.88) | 🟢 **REPURPOSING-READY** — fosmidomycin-class (DXR reductoisomerase inhibitor; INTERVENE1; the 1/32 novel-pathogen hit for N. gonorrhoeae) |
| **gyrA/B, parC/E** | DNA gyrase / topo IV; canonical essential | 🟢 **REPURPOSING-READY** — fluoroquinolones (approved) |
| **murB, murG, murF, murD, mraY, ispE** | cell-wall + MEP cores; BESTINT1 top-10 (0.80–0.91); exp-essential 2–3/3 orgs; resistance+condition-robust | 🟡 **UNDRUGGED, HIGH-VALUE** — validated broad-spectrum cores with NO existing drug → the highest-value novel-chemistry antibacterial targets the programme nominates |

**Fastest real-world path (Arm A):** the undrugged cores (murB/murG/murF/mraY/ispE) are the flagship *novel* antibacterial
targets — multi-axis-validated, broad-spectrum, resistance-robust — and are exactly where a medicinal-chemistry or
fragment-screening effort would have the most validated justification.

## Arm B — Cancer selective-dependency targets
*Validated as selective (non-pan-essential) dependencies (DEPEND1: recover known targets 0.80, generalize to held-out lines
0.80); patient-tumour-driver relevant (F3CLIN1, survives study-bias); intervention status (INTERVENE2).*

| Target | Validation (committed) | Intervention readiness |
|---|---|---|
| **BRAF, KRAS, EGFR, ERBB2, PIK3CA, CDK4, CDK6, BCL2, MAP2K1** | selective dependency + patient driver; INTERVENE2 canonical recovery 10/10 | 🟢 **REPURPOSING-READY** — approved targeted drugs exist (vemurafenib/sotorasib/erlotinib/…); a functional-precision-medicine group can test dependency→drug matching directly |
| **MDM2** | selective dependency (TP53-WT context); patient driver | 🟢 **REPURPOSING-READY** — investigational (phase 3, nutlin/idasanutlin class) |
| **~192 undrugged selective drivers** (80% of the 240 driver-dependencies) | selective dependency + patient driver (DEPEND1/F3CLIN1) | 🟡 **UNDRUGGED, HIGH-VALUE** — validated, patient-relevant, NO existing drug → the highest-value novel-oncology-chemistry targets (the 93%-undrugged ceiling, INTERVENE2) |

**Fastest real-world path (Arm B):** the drugged drivers are an immediate functional-precision-medicine test (does a cell
line's selective dependency predict its response to that target's drug? — note this is the *dependency-match* hypothesis,
distinct from the tested-negative transcriptomic *response-prediction* line). The undrugged driver dependencies are the
flagship novel-oncology targets.

**Synthetic-lethal supplement (INTERVENE3) — a narrow set of drugged combination hypotheses for otherwise-undruggable
targets:** ~0.5–1% of undruggable dependencies have a validated *drugged* SL partner (PARP/BRCA-style). Most credible
existing-drug combination *hypotheses* (in-silico, need a wet-lab combination test): **CCND2→CCND1** (CDK4/6–cyclinD),
**PIK3R2→PIK3R1**, **RPL22L1→RPL22**, **CUL4B→CUL4A**. High-value where they exist, but not a scalable route.

## The single honest prioritization the whole programme yields
**Validated × drugged → test now (no chemistry needed):** murA/fosfomycin, dxr/fosmidomycin, gyrase/fluoroquinolone
(bacteria); BRAF/KRAS/EGFR/BCL2/CDK4-6/PIK3CA (cancer). These are direct, fundable, real-world experiments a lab can run
against a validated, provenance-tagged rationale.
**Validated × undrugged → the highest-value novel-chemistry frontier:** the cell-wall/MEP cores (bacteria) and the ~192
undrugged selective driver-dependencies (cancer). These are *where the validated science says novel drug discovery should
aim* — and they are the F4 de-novo-chemistry / experimental frontier that computation has mapped but cannot itself cross.

## Scope (binds every row)
Targets are computational hypotheses with full provenance and evidence tiers, validated as *essentiality/dependency
enrichment* and *target relevance* — NOT as validated drugs, NOT drug-response prediction (tested-negative), NOT clinical.
"Drugged/repurposing-ready" means an approved/known ligand hits the target (a fast experimental test), not that it is
efficacious or safe for a new indication. Realizing any of this is a wet-lab/clinical action, gated on new experimental
information. This dossier's job is to point that action at the most-validated place.
