# GENETICS1 — Pre-registration (v2, FROZEN before final scoring)

**Arm:** Wave-B human-disease coverage — the third and largest human paradigm: **common / complex / polygenic disease** (T2D, CAD, IBD, RA, asthma, schizophrenia, …). Complements somatic **cancer** (DEPEND1) and germline **monogenic** (MENDEL1).

**Capability under test (NOT new genetics):** compose **human genetics** (GWAS→gene via Open Targets genetic association / L2G) as a **popularity-CONFOUND-CONTROLLED** complex-disease target-relevance arm, and bound it honestly. Prior: Nelson 2015 (Nat Genet) — genetically-supported targets approve ~2× more often. The contribution is (a) replicating that on our fixed setup and (b) the decisive DEPEND1/F3CLIN1 test: does genetics beat a **study-attention / popularity null**?

## Correction log (full transparency — v1 → v2)
v1 defined the universe as **top-500 targets per disease by OT overall score**. `overall` is a collider of predictor (genetic) and outcome (clinical); conditioning on it induces **Berkson bias** → v1 produced OR **0.07** (spurious inversion), an artifact, not biology. v2 **corrects the universe to genome-wide** (all 20,596 NCBI protein-coding genes; a gene absent from a disease's OT association list has genetic=0 & clinical=0 — a true double-negative, since any gene with genetic>0 OR clinical>0 is necessarily listed). The gate NUMBERS from the directive are unchanged; only the invalid universe was fixed, on principle (documented, not tuned). A second bug was caught pre-freeze: an intermediate wrongly set pub=0 for unlisted genes (crammed them into the lowest fame decile → spurious MH-OR 0.70). v2 uses **real** NCBI publication counts for every gene.

### Variables (frozen roles — no leakage)
- **PREDICTOR — `genetic_association`** (OT genetic datatype: L2G/GWAS Catalog/UK Biobank + ClinVar/G2P/Orphanet; **evidence-based, NOT text-mined**). Binary "genetically supported" = `genetic_association > 0` (untunable, most-permissive cut) is the PRIMARY; full dose-response reported at thresholds {0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5}; continuous score used in the logistic.
- **OUTCOME/TRUTH — `clinical`** (OT clinical-precedence = ChEMBL known drug for THIS indication). `drug_target := clinical > 0`. Robustness cut `clinical ≥ 0.5`.
- **POPULARITY CONFOUNDER (PRIMARY) — gene-level publication count** `log1p(pub)` from NCBI gene2pubmed (a gene's general research "fame", not disease-specific — the directive's named control). **SECONDARY confounder** — OT `literature` (disease-specific text-mining co-mention); reported too, with the caveat it is partly a mediator/collider of the outcome (papers are generated BY drug programs & GWAS hits), so it OVER-controls.

## Pre-registered gates (numbers fixed before final cross-tab; wording matches the directive)
Primary unit = (disease, gene) pair.
- **G1 — Nelson replication:** pooled **Fisher OR > 2 AND p < 0.01** at `genetic_association > 0`, genome-wide; corroborated by (i) Mantel–Haenszel OR stratified **by disease** (respects disease structure) and (ii) a random-gene permutation null (K=10000, seed=42) and (iii) the dose-response monotonicity.
- **G2 — DECISIVE popularity/study-bias null (retain SIGNIFICANT enrichment BEYOND popularity):** on the PRIMARY confounder (gene publication count):
  - **(2a)** fame-decile **matched-permutation null** (permute genetic-support labels within `log1p(pub)` deciles, K=10000, seed=42): observed sel∩drug overlap emp **p < 0.01**.
  - **(2b)** **Mantel–Haenszel OR stratified by fame decile**: **MH-OR > 1 with CMH p < 0.01** (enrichment *retained* beyond fame). Report the value + whether it clears the stricter OR>2 bar and at which genetic threshold (dose-response).
  - **(2c)** **multivariable logistic** `drug ~ genetic_association + log1p(pub) (+ disease FE)`, and jointly `+ literature`: require the **genetic coefficient > 0 with p < 0.01** in BOTH.
  - **G2 PASS iff (2a) AND (2b) AND (2c)** — i.e. genetics keeps a significant positive effect beyond popularity. Also report the literature-adjusted MH + logistic as the (over-controlling) secondary.
- **G3 — cross-disease generalization (supporting):** leave-one-disease-out fame-adjusted logistic — genetic coef **> 0 & p < 0.05 in ≥ 24/27 folds**; plus per-disease crude Fisher OR (fraction OR>1).

## Overall verdict rule (frozen)
- **PASS — the third human-disease arm, popularity-controlled** = **G1 AND G2** (genetics replicates Nelson AND retains a significant independent effect beyond study-attention). Report honestly the *magnitude of attenuation* by popularity (crude vs fame-adjusted OR) — a PASS that is partly attenuated is still reported as attenuated, unlike F3CLIN1 whose signal ROSE under stratification.
- **NEGATIVE — first-class honest bound** = G1 fails **OR** G2 fails (crude enrichment fully explained by popularity → mirrors the human-single-disease ceiling). Reported plainly; NOT tuned to pass.

## Scope / honesty (frozen — binds every claim)
In-silico secondary analysis of a cross-sectional Open Targets 26.06 snapshot; this is **TARGET-RELEVANCE via genetics** (like F3CLIN1 for cancer) — **NOT** drug-response prediction, **NOT** a molecule, **NOT** clinical efficacy/outcome. Contribution = a popularity-confound-controlled complex-disease router arm + honest abstention where genetic evidence is absent. Intervention still hits the affinity wall (HIT2/B49/B65). **Causal caveat (both directions):** popularity proxies are partly colliders on drug-status (drug-targets are more published), so fame/literature adjustment likely UNDER-estimates the true genetic effect → the popularity-free effect is bounded between the crude and adjusted ORs; and a fully clean test of the Nelson causal claim requires a *temporal/prospective* design not runnable on this snapshot. Residual `clinical`↔drug-development circularity flagged; ALS/epilepsy are genetically heterogeneous. Reproduce ×2 byte-identical over sorted-key JSON payload (excludes verdict/provenance).
