## What it demonstrates
The fullest vision working as **one honest system**: the composite fires only the signal(s) whose transfer
condition is **validated** for the input's biology, at calibrated/capped confidence, and **abstains** where none
transfers. **"Any disease" = honest DECISION coverage** — a real answer where a signal transfers, an explicit
abstention where none does — **not a universal model.** It neither over-claims ("works for any disease") nor
under-claims ("bacteria only"); it applies what is validated per biology and refuses where it is not. The
abstention on the novel zero-screen parasite is the integrity core: the system says "I don't have a validated
signal for this" rather than emit a confident-looking wrong answer.
