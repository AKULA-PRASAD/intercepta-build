When a new or neglected pathogen — or any disease — presents with **zero activity data**, how far can a computational system
get toward credible intervention targets from sequence, structure, and transferable knowledge alone, and where does it break?
We built pre-registered, reproduced-twice, open-data experiments that map this frontier honestly. For target identification we
establish a well-controlled negative (homology does not beat a conservation null and can fail silently) and the one signal that
breaks the ceiling: mechanistic **flux-balance gene-essentiality**, enriched for *experimental* gene-knockout essentiality
across six curated metabolic models (odds ratios 5–64). In a **prospective-blind suite across all three domains of life**
(never-seen organisms, predictions locked before the answer was consulted) it reveals a sharp, honest **prokaryote/eukaryote
split (4 pass / 3 fail)**: every prokaryote with an adequate model passes — three bacterial phyla and, crossing into a second
domain, an **archaeon** — while failures fall *predictably* on invariant/model boundaries (a host-scavenging kinetoplastid; a
sparse de-novo model; a fungus with real but sub-gate enrichment, p≈4×10⁻⁵). Predicting on a never-seen domain yet failing
coherently along biological boundaries is the signature of genuine generalization, not memorization.

We formalize this as a **transfer-condition law**: each label-free signal transfers only as far as the biological invariant it
rides on is conserved. Viruses (no metabolism): sequence homology fails, but blind **structural** homology recovers the correct
drugged-enzyme class across five viruses. Human cancer: a **functional-dependency** signal recovers known targets, generalizes
to held-out cell lines, and is enriched for patient-tumour drivers after study-bias correction. Two load-bearing **negatives**
bound the work: that dependency signal does not transfer label-free to a zero-screen organism, and patient drug-response
prediction fails external replication. These validated signals compose into an explicit **biology-class-aware router** that
applies what is validated and **abstains** where none is — "any disease" as honest decision coverage, not a universal model. No
claim is clinical; the remaining distance to a drug is gated by new experimental information, not more computation.
